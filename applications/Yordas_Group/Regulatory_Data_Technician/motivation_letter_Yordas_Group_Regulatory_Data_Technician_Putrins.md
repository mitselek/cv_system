**Subject: Application for Regulatory Data Technician**

Dear Hiring Manager,

I am applying for the Regulatory Data Technician position with seven years of data curation experience, extensive Python and Pandas expertise, and a proven track record in large-scale data extraction and processing.

I have processed 108,867 museum records using Pandas 2.0+ across a 38-file CSV ETL pipeline for the Vabamu museum migration project. This work required meticulous attention to detail, data quality assurance, and handling inconsistent source data - challenges similar to those in your position with varying government and industry data sources.

I developed and maintained an 800-line BeautifulSoup system for extracting data from the Eventival platform as Development Lead for the Institute of Beautiful Code on PÖFF (Black Nights Film Festival). This system handled XML, HTML, and JSON data formats with robust error handling and validation, operating reliably during critical festival periods. I have also built multi-platform job monitoring scrapers for Duunitori and CV Keskus. This experience directly supports your need to extract substance data from PDFs, HTML tables, and searchable databases with varying quality standards.

I bring advanced spreadsheet proficiency (Google Sheets 10/10, Excel 8/10) and substantial database experience. At the Estonian Institute of Historical Memory, I trained historians to work directly with MySQL databases. I have production experience with PostgreSQL (PÖFF platform), MySQL (memory institution data), and Oracle/PL/SQL (Estonian Ministry of Justice systems).

While I have extensive Pandas experience, I have not used Numpy in my documented projects - the concepts are similar and readily learnable. I do not have direct experience with chemical regulations, but I am genuinely interested in sustainability and the critical role that structured regulatory data plays in protecting public health and the environment. Working with historians and subject matter experts has taught me to rapidly acquire domain knowledge and make informed decisions about data handling.

I am available for an interview at your convenience.
